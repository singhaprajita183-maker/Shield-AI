# 🛡️ Shield AI: Proactive Women Safety & Emergency Companion

**Shield AI** is an intelligent, proactive safety companion designed to transform urban personal security from reactive emergency calls to predictive, instant protection for women.

Developed by **Aprajita Singh** (Class 10 Solo Developer) for **Girls Hack Day Delhi**.

## 🌟 Key Features
- 🎙️ **Voice-Activated SOS Trigger**: Instant alert dispatch without manually clicking or touching the phone.
- 🗺️ **Predictive Safety Heatmaps**: Real-time localized risk evaluation and danger zone alerts.
- 📡 **Automated Emergency Dispatch**: Live location sharing with trusted contacts & local authorities.
- 🛡️ **Proactive Companion Mode**: Smart AI monitoring that predicts potential safety risks.
