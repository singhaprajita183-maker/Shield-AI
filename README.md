## 🌟 Vision & Impact

Traditional safety apps rely heavily on manual triggers—requiring a user to press a button or call during an active crisis. **Shield AI** bridges this critical gap by acting as an intelligent, proactive safety companion that continuously evaluates ambient risk, enables zero-touch distress triggering, and provides instant emergency intelligence to trusted contacts and authorities.

Designed and developed by **Aprajita Singh** (Class 10 Solo Developer).

---

## 🔥 Key Features

* 🎙️ **Voice-Activated SOS Trigger:** Zero-touch distress dispatch mechanism that eliminates the need to physically open or interact with the phone during high-risk situations.
* 🗺️ **Predictive Safety Heatmaps:** Evaluates localized safety indicators and notifies users proactive danger-zone alerts before entering unsafe areas.
* 📡 **Automated Emergency Dispatch:** Real-time location streaming and instant distress signals broadcasted directly to designated emergency contacts.
* 🛡️ **Proactive Companion Mode:** Continuous background intelligence that monitors travel routes and flags unusual delays or route anomalies.
